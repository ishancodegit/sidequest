Questions

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It runs in Safari or Chrome on any modern phone. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen like a normal app.

Is my camera video uploaded anywhere?

No. The AI rep counter runs entirely on your phone. The video never leaves your device.

Do I need an account?

No account, no email, no sign up. Your progress saves in your browser, and you can export a backup file whenever you want.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep it, including future updates.

Which exercises can the AI count?

Push-ups, squats, pull-ups and pulldowns, burpees, sit-ups, lunges, jumping jacks, dips, curls, overhead press, bench press, rows, lateral raises, deadlifts and hip thrusts. Anything else works with tap counting.

Do I need gym equipment?

No. The Calisthenics and Monk class setups are fully bodyweight, and most AI-tracked exercises need nothing at all.
